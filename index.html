About Cafe Sky

★★★★★ 5.0 (6 reviews)

Cafe Sky is a casual cafe located at Ammara Mall, Nagan Chowrangi, North Karachi.

Enjoy rooftop seating, coffee, halal food, small plates, vegetarian options and late-night food in a relaxed atmosphere.

Perfect for solo dining, families and groups.
